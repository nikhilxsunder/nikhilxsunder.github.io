### Dean of Miami Herbert highlights undergraduate AI research at FLAIRS-39

**Paul A. Pavlou, Dean, University of Miami Herbert Business School** · June 2026

Dean Pavlou highlighted three Miami Herbert undergraduates — Nikhil Sunder, Kyle Pasion, and Charlie Tucker — who presented original AI research at FLAIRS-39 alongside PhD students, supported by Professor Maikel León. His framing: this is what follows from treating undergraduates as researchers and creators of knowledge rather than as learners only.
